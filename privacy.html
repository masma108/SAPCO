This policy describes how SAPCO Construction Group uses information submitted through this website.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Information we collect

When you contact SAPCO, you may provide your name, business name, email address, phone number, project location, expected timeline, project description, and links you choose to share. We also receive communications you send directly by email or phone.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, discuss project requirements, prepare proposals when appropriate, and communicate about potential work. We do not use information submitted through the inquiry form for unrelated marketing without separate permission.

Form processing

The contact form uses Web3Forms, a third-party form processing service. Submitted information is transmitted to that provider so your inquiry can be delivered to SAPCO. Please avoid sending sensitive personal, payment, or confidential project information through the form.

Sharing and retention

We may share information with service providers that help operate the website or manage inquiries, and when required by law. We keep inquiry information as needed for business and recordkeeping purposes; specific retention periods may vary.

Other services and links

The website may load fonts or media from external services and may link to third-party websites. Their own privacy practices apply when you use their services.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, subject to any applicable legal or business recordkeeping obligations. For privacy questions, email sapco.cgroup@gmail.com.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy as the website or its practices change. Updated wording will appear on this page with a new date.
