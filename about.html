OUR STORY

A strong foundation.
A clear commitment.

SAPCO Construction Group is a Texas-based general contractor serving residential, commercial, and public-sector projects. With a foundation in engineering and a focus on construction delivery, the company approaches each project with thoughtful planning and attention to requirements.

Our approach emphasizes communication, coordination of schedules and materials, quality control, and proper documentation—from the early stages of planning through project closeout.

THE FOUNDER

THE PERSON BEHIND SAPCO

Siar Paiman

Founder · Structural Engineer in Training (EIT)

Siar earned a B.S. in Civil Engineering from The University of Texas at Arlington and works in structural bridge engineering. His professional background brings a careful, methodical approach to planning, problem solving, and attention to detail.

He founded SAPCO Construction Group to apply that perspective to construction projects while prioritizing reliability, clear communication, and a strong client experience.

General contractor specializing in new construction, rehabilitation, and infrastructure improvements for residential, commercial, and public-sector clients.
